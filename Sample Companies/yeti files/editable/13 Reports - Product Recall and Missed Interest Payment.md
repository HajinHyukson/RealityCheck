## E01 Incident and operating evidence

Management immediately halted shipments of the affected lots and initiated a recall. The affected family had represented 70% of the fictional Borrower's sales during the prior three months. This report describes no real YETI product, SKU, recall or regulator action.

These were future orders, not recognized sales or receivables; their cancellation is not a second write-off of recorded revenue. The customers require independent clearance before placing replacement orders. No clearance had been received by May 10, and the replacement-production window was not expected before July.

April net sales were $4.0m and landed cost of units sold was $3.8m, giving gross profit of $0.2m and gross margin of 5.00%. That noncash inventory loss is reported separately from the $3.8m cost of sold units. The existing $5.0m supplier advance remains outstanding and is separate from the owned inventory and bank cash.
