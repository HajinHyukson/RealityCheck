## Receipts, payments and funding assumptions
Supplier confirmation YETI-SUP-0217, received February 17, requires $5.0m on March 5 to retain production slots for an existing ordinary replenishment order. This amount was previously due May 28 and was included on that date in management's purchasing schedule. Management accepted the revised supplier payment schedule on February 18. It is an ordinary procurement payment, not new discretionary expansion or borrowed money.

The prepayment is a supplier advance until ownership of the goods transfers; it is not immediately COGS or eligible cash. The full $5.0m moves from May to March, so cumulative cash is $5.0m lower through May 27 and rejoins the prior schedule after May 28. This is separate from the $2.4m supplier payment already deferred to April 30 in report 09. That payment remains included once, on April 30.

The supplier declined another deferral in its February 18 confirmation. Releasing these production slots would interrupt committed customer orders; no substitute supplier with the same delivery window is available. Inventory reductions and collection gains from report 09 are already included. No equity injection or replacement borrowing is committed.

All other forecast receipts, fees, operating costs, interest, estimated cash-tax installments and capital expenditure assumptions remain as in the comparison schedule. The prior operating mitigations are already included. No new financing, owner contribution or discretionary expansion is assumed. Estimated earnings, receivables and supplier advances are kept separate from cleared bank cash. Amounts below are USD millions.
