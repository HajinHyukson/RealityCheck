## Assumption and risk assessment
Supported means current evidence remains consistent with the approved claim. Weakened means there is a challenge but its extent or persistence is unresolved. Contradicted means direct evidence materially conflicts with the claim. Insufficient evidence means a reliable conclusion is unavailable. Record credit direction separately: adverse, beneficial, mixed, neutral or unclear. An operating assumption can fail while financial covenants pass.

## Causality and double counting
An industry article can motivate an exposure check; it does not prove borrower losses or contract failure. Trace earnings, working-capital movements and cash separately. Avoid counting one delayed receipt both as a permanent revenue loss and an additional permanent cash loss. Distinguish committed cost reductions, uncommitted plans, cash already received and future estimates.

## Suggested responses
Keep routine evidence in history when no material change is supported. Ask for missing receipts, cohort data, inventory or contract evidence where necessary. Consider focused monitoring and revised forecasts before proposing new terms. A candidate credit agreement must identify a persistent protection gap, affected clause and required approval. Do not alter history or relax thresholds to hide a past failure.

## Comparison and decision record
Use the same guidelines for an origination-only control and a candidate incorporating later reports. Changes already preferred using the original evidence are not caused by new information. Preserve counterevidence and explain any remaining uncertainty. Only signed-off fictional amendments under C10 become operative. A model cannot approve a waiver, cure or enforcement step.
