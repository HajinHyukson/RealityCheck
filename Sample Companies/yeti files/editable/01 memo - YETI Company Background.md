## Business facts
YETI designs and sells outdoor products including coolers, equipment and drinkware. It reaches consumers through direct channels and wholesale partners. Third parties manufacture products and provide logistics. The filing discusses supplier concentration, timely imports, transportation costs and inventory management. Product costs and inbound freight affect cost of goods sold, while distribution and fulfillment costs also affect profitability.
