Paid accounts roll forward as opening accounts less expiring accounts plus renewed accounts plus converted trials. Subscription revenue uses the average of opening and ending paid accounts, multiplied by $15. This is an explicit simplified recognition convention. Gross subscription billings use the number of new and renewed annual contracts multiplied by $180. Processing fees are expensed against recognized subscription revenue and paid against billings; the base case has equal billings and recognized revenue, so no processing-cost timing adjustment arises. Other receipts and operating costs settle in the same month. Processor receivables, prepaid assets, payables and deferred revenue remain unchanged in the base case. No refunds, family plans, foreign exchange, dividends or extra debt are assumed.

## Definitions and reconciliation
Base monthly EBITDA is $15.75m minus $2.52m processing fees, $3.15m hosting and $7.08m other operating expense. Monthly income tax is 25% of EBITDA less $0.5m depreciation and $0.45m interest. Cash increases by EBITDA less interest, cash tax and capital expenditure. Depreciation is noncash. Net fixed assets increase by capital expenditure less depreciation, and equity increases by net income. Opening balance-sheet balance and every forecast balance-sheet balance reconcile in the workbook.

The closing illustrative Net Leverage is ($60m minus $38m) divided by $36m, or 0.6111 times. The contractual limit is 2.50 times. The cash floor is $25m; a forecast below $32m creates a separate reporting obligation. No later report is embedded in this model.
