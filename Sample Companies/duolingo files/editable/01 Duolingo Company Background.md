## Public source
This digest summarizes Duolingo Inc's Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025, filed February 27, 2026. It is a selected business-background digest, not the full filing or a lender underwriting memo.

## Business facts
Duolingo provides a learning platform with free access and optional paid features. Its revenue sources include subscriptions, advertising, English proficiency testing and in-app purchases. Annual subscription payments can arrive before the related revenue is recognized. Mobile distribution and collections depend substantially on third-party platforms, including Apple and Google. The filing discusses competition for learners' attention and spending, payment timing, hosting costs and willingness to pay.

## References
Item 1 Business describes the freemium model. Item 1A Risk Factors discusses third-party platforms and competition. Note 2 Revenue Recognition and Cost of Revenues explains subscription revenue, deferred revenue and processing fees.
