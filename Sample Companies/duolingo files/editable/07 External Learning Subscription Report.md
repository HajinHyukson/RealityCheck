SYNTHETIC DEMO. The research desk, survey and events are entirely fictional. No actual research was conducted. This is not a report of observed market developments.

## Research scope
This entirely fictional industry note describes a synthetic survey conducted November 20 to November 30, 2026. It does not report actual market research or identify any individual company. The sample consists of 1,200 adult users of digital learning tools in a simulated online panel. It is not weighted to represent a national population or any platform's customer base.

## Survey observations
| Response | Respondents | Denominator |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Used a general-purpose free AI tool for language practice in the prior month | 360 | 1200 |
| Said they intended to reduce spending on paid learning tools in the next three months | 264 | 1200 |
| Said a structured curriculum remained important when choosing a paid tool | 732 | 1200 |

Responses can overlap; they are not mutually exclusive market-share categories. Among the 360 respondents who used free AI tools, 216 also reported using a dedicated learning application. The survey did not collect payment records, observe cancellations, identify annual renewal dates or measure successful learning outcomes.

## Possible implications
Free alternatives could make it harder for a learning service to convert trials into paid purchases. They could also complement a structured paid service. Increased engagement therefore has no unambiguous implication for revenue. The direction depends on the provider's offering, customer cohorts, pricing and retention.

## Information needed for company assessment
Assess exposure using the provider's finalized trial conversion, annual renewal cohorts, paid-account definitions, billings, cash collections and service costs. Survey intention is not a realized customer cancellation. No change in app-store terms, payment-processing fees or any company's contract is reported here. The note does not establish a breach of any credit agreement.
