## Business facts
Planet Fitness operates a fitness business with Franchise, Corporate-owned clubs and Equipment segments. Franchise revenue includes royalties and other franchise-related fees. Corporate-owned clubs earn membership and related revenue, while the Equipment segment sells equipment to franchisees. The filing distinguishes the economics of franchisees from those of the public company. Member demand, club operating expenses, franchisee performance and investment in equipment affect the business.
