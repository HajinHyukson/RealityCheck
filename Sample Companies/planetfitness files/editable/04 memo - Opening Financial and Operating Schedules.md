## Statement reconciliation
Income tax expense is 25% of positive pretax income. Cash-tax installments follow the fixed $0.066250m monthly origination schedule; any difference accumulates in tax payable or receivable. Fixed assets change by capital expenditure less depreciation, and equity by net income. No dividends, extra borrowings or unlisted cash flows are assumed. The workbook reconciles cash, working capital and the balance sheet in each month. Forecast cash is not a C01 actual measurement; C02 requires actual LTM EBITDA.
