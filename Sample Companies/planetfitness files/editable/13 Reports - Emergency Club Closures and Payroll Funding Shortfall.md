## E01 Incident and operating evidence
Engineering survey PLNT-ENG-0603, received June 3, documents failed roof connections at twelve of the fictional operator's twenty leased clubs. The same construction detail was present at all twelve sites. Management closed these clubs to public access on June 4 after receiving the engineer's written recommendation. Reopening requires remediation and an independent sign-off; contractors estimate at least eight weeks. There is no confirmed reopening date. The other eight clubs remain open.

The affected twelve clubs served 60,000 of the operator's 96,000 active, billable accounts at May 31. By June 11, 20,000 of the affected accounts had canceled and the other 40,000 were frozen with billing suspended. The unaffected clubs retained 36,000 billable accounts. Thus 96,000 opening accounts less 20,000 cancellations and 40,000 freezes equals 36,000 current billable accounts. Frozen accounts are not counted again as cancellations. These are June 1-11 observations, not finalized full-month attrition or collection rates.

The sites remain leased and require security, minimum staffing and remedial work while closed. This structural incident is separate from the five-club ventilation and water-damage repairs completed in May. No earlier repair cost or equipment invoice is charged a second time.
