# 02 Original Credit Agreement

Document PLNT-02 | Available 2026-10-01
Prepared by Scenario Lender P01 and fictional borrower

SYNTHETIC DEMO. Planet Fitness Demo Club Operator LLC and Scenario Lender P01 are invented. No affiliation, financing or guarantee by Planet Fitness, Inc. is asserted. All financial figures, terms and scenario events below are fictional.

## Parties and status
Facility PLNT-DEMO-01, version 1.0. Borrower: Planet Fitness Demo Club Operator LLC. Lender: Scenario Lender P01. The fictional parties approve this simplified agreement effective October 1, 2026 solely for the simulation. Planet Fitness, Inc. is not a party, guarantor or source of repayment. No real signature or legal obligation is created.

## C00 Facility and payment terms
Advance $18.0000m on October 1, 2026. Refinance $15.0000m prior debt and retain the remainder as liquidity. The facility is unsecured, has no guarantee and no fees, and bears fixed annual interest of 9.00% using twelve equal monthly periods. Monthly interest is $0.135000m, payable on the last Business Day. Principal is due October 1, 2029 without earlier scheduled amortization. Voluntary prepayment is permitted without penalty with two Business Days' notice; there is no reborrowing. Interest on prepaid principal runs through the payment date.

## Measurement perimeter and definitions
Only the fictional Borrower's assets, liabilities and earnings count. USD millions are used unless stated. Indebtedness includes drawn borrowed-money principal and excludes ordinary trade payables, undrawn commitments and operating lease liabilities. Lease rent remains an expense in EBITDA. The model is a simplified management presentation, not a complete GAAP lease-accounting model.

Unrestricted Cash means cleared USD bank deposits owned by the Borrower and freely available for operating payments. Exclude receivables, inventory, cash in transit, other entities' funds and restricted deposits. The $1.0000m processor or landlord reserve is an asset but is excluded from eligible cash and debt netting.

EBITDA is actual recognized revenue less all operating costs before interest, income taxes, depreciation and amortization for the trailing twelve months. No freight, marketing, opening, recruitment, AI or other operating-cost add-backs are allowed. Net Leverage is MAX(0, Indebtedness minus Unrestricted Cash) divided by this EBITDA. Nonpositive EBITDA fails C02; it never creates a negative compliant ratio. Use unrounded figures when testing, with a numerical tolerance of $0.01 only for arithmetic rounding.

A member is one active, billable individual account in the fictional 20-club group, excluding freezes and cancellations effective by month-end. Attrition is departures during a calendar month divided by opening active members. Joins are separate from departures. Current Dues Collection Rate is cleared cash applied to dues billed for that month divided by those dues; prior arrears recovered are excluded. A failed payment is not automatically a cancellation. Dues revenue uses average opening and closing members times the monthly price, an explicit simplified convention. There are no annual prepayments or enrollment fees in this scenario. Other service revenue is separately recognized and collected monthly.

## C01 Minimum actual cash
Unrestricted Cash must be at least $6.0000m at each calendar month-end starting October 31, 2026. Test the actual balance on the measurement date. The date never rolls because of a weekend. Supply a total-bank-to-eligible-cash reconciliation. Equality is compliant.

## C02 Maximum actual leverage
Net Leverage must not exceed 3.00 times on March 31, June 30, September 30 and December 31, starting December 31, 2026. Provide the actual twelve-month revenue and cost components, debt and eligible cash. A single month annualized, a forecast, or an unrelated public company's EBITDA cannot substitute for the required denominator.

## C03 Monthly reporting
Deliver the management income and cash schedules, operating metrics and working-capital reconciliations within twenty calendar days after month-end, by 5 pm New York time. Roll a non-Business-Day delivery deadline to the next Business Day. For quarter ends include C02's actual trailing-twelve-month components. Retain original assumptions when later results differ.

## C04 Operating deterioration notice
If monthly Attrition exceeds 4.50%, or Current Dues Collection Rate is below 94.00%, notify the Lender within three Business Days after the finance officer receives finalized results. Include the measured components, date, explanation and contrary evidence. A zero denominator is unassessed and must be explained. These thresholds trigger notice; they are not minimum operating-performance covenants. Timely notice satisfies the reporting duty without changing the underlying result.

## C05 Forecast and weekly reporting
If a dated management forecast projects any weekly Unrestricted Cash balance below $6.5000m in the next thirteen weeks, deliver that forecast and an action plan within five Business Days after the finance officer receives it. Starting the first Friday after initial delivery, send a refreshed forecast every Friday by 5 pm New York time, rolling delivery to the next Business Day when required. Continue until two successive delivered forecasts, dated at least seven days apart, show all thirteen weeks at or above the trigger. A forecast trigger is separate from C01's actual-cash test.

## C06 Expansion and distributions
While C05 reporting is active, no new discretionary expansion commitment above $0.7500m, alone or in related transactions, may be entered without written lender consent. Ordinary payroll, rent, procurement and already-contracted payments are excluded. No shareholder distributions while any financial failure is uncured or C05 reporting is active. An uncommitted plan does not itself establish a prohibited transaction.

## C07 Borrowing and security
No additional borrowed money or lien securing borrowed money without prior written lender consent. Ordinary supplier credit is permitted if payment is contractually due within 180 days and the supplier has agreed to the term; unpaid invoices beyond the agreed date are not an authorized extension. The disclosed restricted reserve and ordinary leases are permitted. Neither a supplier deferral nor an AI recommendation amends this agreement.

## C08 Accuracy and financial-failure notices
Provide measurement dates, availability dates, responsible scenario roles and definitions. Correct a known material reporting error within two Business Days and retain the original. Notify the Lender of a known C01 or C02 failure within two Business Days after the finance officer receives the finalized test. Missing information does not establish compliance.

## C09 Defaults and cures
Unpaid principal or interest becomes an Event of Default after five Business Days without payment. A C01 or C02 failure becomes an Event of Default only after written lender notice specifying the failure and ten Business Days without cure or waiver. A C03-C08 failure requires written lender notice and five Business Days without cure. Insolvency is an immediate Event of Default. For C01, a cure requires a certified current balance at or above the floor and written lender acknowledgment. A later forecast alone is insufficient. The historical test remains recorded. The Lender may accelerate a continuing Event of Default by written notice; a risk flag or forecast alone is not an Event of Default.

## C10 Amendments and waivers
Only an instrument expressly approved by both fictional parties can amend this agreement or waive a failure. It must identify clauses, approval and effective dates and any historical waiver. Drafts and model proposals have no operative effect. The opening set has no amendment or waiver.

## C11 Calendar and delivery
Business Days are Monday-Friday excluding November 26 and December 25, 2026, and January 1, January 18, February 15 and May 31, 2027. Later dates require the agreed scenario calendar before testing. Count Business Days from the day after the triggering date. All delivery times are New York time. Delivery occurs on the recorded receipt in the fictional lender portal. No additional material-adverse-change or cross-default clause is implied.
