# Evidentary Legal Systems, Inc. Underwriting memorandum

Memo B09-UW-2026-01. Synthetic approval and availability: 2026-12-31. Proposed for a private-credit analyst. Dollar amounts are millions. Future scenarios are forecasts known at origination, not later observations.

## Borrower and credit decision

Evidentary provides hosted legal-hold, document collection, review, and production software to litigation boutiques and corporate legal departments, billing subscriptions plus matter usage. Its risk centers on defensible chain of custody, privilege controls, and matter-volume variability that can reduce usage revenue quickly.

The 4.20x leverage maximum permits about a 22% EBITDA decline from 3.26x opening net leverage. A 1.30x annual cash-coverage minimum addresses the combination of usage-sensitive revenue, 67-day collections, capitalized development, cash interest, and scheduled principal. The $7.5 million transaction floor preserves resources for secure hosting and matter migrations. The principal vulnerability is evidentiary integrity: a chain-of-custody or privilege-control failure can impair customer trust and create costly remediation even without immediate subscription termination. Specialized covenants require immutable collection manifests, privileged-review access controls, and controlled deletion after legal-hold release. Acquisition capacity remains limited because migrating acquired matters and custody records creates operational risk that leverage alone does not capture.

### A1

**Claim:** Defensible custody records and controlled privileged access sustain trust with litigation and corporate legal customers.

**Baseline evidence:** The approved underwriting memo's Evidentiary Controls paragraph reviews synthetic manifests, privileged-room settings, and exception records.

**Review criterion:** A material custody gap, hash mismatch, or unauthorized privileged export is identified.

**Contract relationship:** direct; S1, S2, C08. These links describe possible protection and do not add a covenant.

### A2

**Claim:** Matter-level authorization and deletion controls prevent irreversible loss or disclosure of customer evidence.

**Baseline evidence:** The approved underwriting memo's Matter Governance paragraph identifies administrator roles, retention workflows, dual approvals, and deletion certificates.

**Review criterion:** Emergency access is unresolved, export authority fails, or data is deleted without required authorization.

**Contract relationship:** direct; S2, S3, C07. These links describe possible protection and do not add a covenant.

### A3

**Claim:** Subscriptions plus diversified matter usage and 67-day collections provide adequate annual cash coverage.

**Baseline evidence:** The approved underwriting memo's Cash Flow paragraph reconciles subscription and usage invoices, receivable aging, investment, taxes, and debt service.

**Review criterion:** Matter usage falls materially, receivable aging lengthens, or cash coverage approaches its minimum.

**Contract relationship:** direct; C02, C09. These links describe possible protection and do not add a covenant.

### A4

**Claim:** Opening leverage is tolerable if custody systems and customer data remain protected within the collateral group.

**Baseline evidence:** The approved underwriting memo's Capital Structure paragraph presents opening leverage, collateral systems, amortization, and transaction limits.

**Review criterion:** Leverage rises, core systems transfer, or an irreversible matter-data event occurs.

**Contract relationship:** direct; C01, C06, C07, S3. These links describe possible protection and do not add a covenant.

## Financial model conventions

The authored balance sheet records trade receivables of $18.540m at 67 collection days, other operating current assets of $12.547m, net fixed/software assets of $29.200m, and historical acquisition goodwill of $96.600m. These are book balances, not collateral recovery estimates. See the model's explicit prepaid/unbilled and deferred-billing simplifications.

- 2025 is comparative income and balance-sheet data; a 2025 cash-flow statement is not fabricated.
- 2026 historical restructuring expense is spread evenly across the four quarters; it is not forecast to recur in 2027.
- Predecessor debt has the same principal and fixed interest rate, with no 2026 amortization; refinancing at par adds no cash or fees.
- 2026 shareholder distributions or contributions reconcile the historical cash target and precede the new agreement.
- Payables and operating accruals (including accrued expenses and deferred customer billings) are held constant; receivables and other operating current assets absorb the stated working-capital change. Deferred customer billings are not forecast separately.
- Other operating current assets comprise prepaid expenses and unbilled contract assets; model movements are explicit, not new EBITDA addbacks.
- Historical goodwill represents earlier acquisitions and stays constant; fixed/software assets roll by capex less depreciation/amortization.
- Restricted cash is borrower-owned cash reserved by contract for operating performance obligations, not client money; it has no matching client-funds liability and no competing lien. It is excluded from leverage netting and available cash.
- Forecast negative cash is an unfunded deficit, not an actual bank balance or assumed new loan; such a scenario is infeasible without corrective financing or spending changes.
- Cash tax is 25% of positive pretax income, with no loss benefit or deferred-tax complexity.
- Q1 realizations are synthetic, with no assumed submission timestamp; every actual delivery obligation must be tested separately.
- Annual interest uses ACT/365F; principal installments are effective December 31, so within-year principal is constant.
