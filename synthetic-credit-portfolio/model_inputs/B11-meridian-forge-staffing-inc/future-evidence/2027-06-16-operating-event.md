# Meridian Forge Staffing, Inc. Executed Receivables Purchase Agreement and CFO Memorandum

Synthetic document B11-EV-2027-01. Event date: 2027-06-15. Available to analyst: 2027-06-16.

## P1

On June 15, 2027, Meridian Forge executed a receivables purchase agreement transferring ownership of selected staffing invoices to Flint Harbor Funding LLC for immediate cash; the first invoice schedule was attached and effective upon signature.

## P2

The CFO stated on June 16 that the arrangement is nonrecourse and was labeled a sale rather than debt, but provided no lender consent and confirmed that customer remittances on transferred invoices will be directed to Flint Harbor.
