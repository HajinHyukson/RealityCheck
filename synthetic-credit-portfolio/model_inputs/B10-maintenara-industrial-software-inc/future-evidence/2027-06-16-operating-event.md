# Maintenara Industrial Software, Inc. Red Basin plant alert-interruption incident

Synthetic document B10-EV-2027-01. Event date: 2027-06-15. Available to analyst: 2027-06-16.

## P1

Monitoring records dated June 15, 2027 show that severity-one alerts for Red Basin assets designated critical in the applicable implementation schedule were disabled for seven consecutive days after an expired notification credential. The responsible operations officer learned of the interruption at 8:10 a.m. on June 15; no planned-maintenance notice or alternate escalation path existed.

## P2

Management reported June 16 that the credential was renewed, alert delivery was restored that afternoon, the customer and Lender were notified, and a credential-expiry monitor was added. Management identified no equipment loss during the interruption.
