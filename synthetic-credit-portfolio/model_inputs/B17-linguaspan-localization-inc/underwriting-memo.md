# LinguaSpan Localization, Inc. Underwriting memorandum

Memo B17-UW-2026-01. Synthetic approval and availability: 2026-12-31. Proposed for a private-credit analyst. Dollar amounts are millions. Future scenarios are forecasts known at origination, not later observations.

## Borrower and credit decision

LinguaSpan translates technical, legal-support, and software content for enterprise clients through recurring language programs and project assignments. Margins depend on linguist quality and workflow automation, while confidential source content can be exposed by uncontrolled subcontracting or public machine-learning tools.

Opening net leverage is 2.87x on $15.0 million of Covenant EBITDA, and the 3.70x maximum allows about a 23% EBITDA decline at constant net debt. A 2.50x interest-coverage minimum supplements leverage because low physical capital does not protect against rapid customer loss, while current earnings provide meaningful opening coverage. The $5 million transaction floor supports payroll and linguist payments through the 48-day collection cycle. Recurring programs offer visibility, but project mix and confidentiality failures can alter renewal behavior quickly. Underwriting depends on qualified linguists, controlled subcontracting, and protection of source content and translation memories. The main vulnerability is a workflow shortcut that exposes confidential material to an unapproved external tool, producing reputational damage before financial reporting captures churn.

## Financial model conventions

The authored balance sheet records trade receivables of $7.627m at 48 collection days, other operating current assets of $7.095m, net fixed/software assets of $12.300m, and historical acquisition goodwill of $57.500m. These are book balances, not collateral recovery estimates. See the model's explicit prepaid/unbilled and deferred-billing simplifications.

- 2025 is comparative income and balance-sheet data; a 2025 cash-flow statement is not fabricated.
- 2026 historical restructuring expense is spread evenly across the four quarters; it is not forecast to recur in 2027.
- Predecessor debt has the same principal and fixed interest rate, with no 2026 amortization; refinancing at par adds no cash or fees.
- 2026 shareholder distributions or contributions reconcile the historical cash target and precede the new agreement.
- Payables and operating accruals (including accrued expenses and deferred customer billings) are held constant; receivables and other operating current assets absorb the stated working-capital change. Deferred customer billings are not forecast separately.
- Other operating current assets comprise prepaid expenses and unbilled contract assets; model movements are explicit, not new EBITDA addbacks.
- Historical goodwill represents earlier acquisitions and stays constant; fixed/software assets roll by capex less depreciation/amortization.
- Restricted cash is borrower-owned cash reserved by contract for operating performance obligations, not client money; it has no matching client-funds liability and no competing lien. It is excluded from leverage netting and available cash.
- Forecast negative cash is an unfunded deficit, not an actual bank balance or assumed new loan; such a scenario is infeasible without corrective financing or spending changes.
- Cash tax is 25% of positive pretax income, with no loss benefit or deferred-tax complexity.
- Q1 realizations are synthetic, with no assumed submission timestamp; every actual delivery obligation must be tested separately.
- Annual interest uses ACT/365F; principal installments are effective December 31, so within-year principal is constant.
