# Clinivanta Admin Systems, Inc. Underwriting memorandum

Memo B02-UW-2026-01. Synthetic approval and availability: 2026-12-31. Proposed for a private-credit analyst. Dollar amounts are millions. Future scenarios are forecasts known at origination, not later observations.

## Borrower and credit decision

Clinivanta provides subscription prior-authorization, eligibility, and denial-workflow software to regional provider groups, with implementation and transaction fees as secondary revenue. Cash flow is exposed to payer-rule changes, protected-health-information controls, and evidence that offshore-free domestic subcontractors follow required access procedures.

The 4.30x maintenance ceiling provides roughly 22% EBITDA decline capacity from 3.35x opening net leverage. A separate $8 million month-end liquidity floor and $11 million forecast trigger address slower provider collections and payroll-heavy implementation costs without treating restricted customer funds as available cash. Three-percent original-principal amortization is supported by recurring subscriptions but leaves meaningful refinancing exposure. The critical vulnerability is operational dependency on changing payer rules and controlled handling of protected health information by staff and subcontractors. Specialized covenants require a traceable rule-update process, quarterly access attestations, and prompt notice of material payer rejections. The transaction cash floor and conservative acquisition test keep integration spending from consuming the liquidity buffer.

## Financial model conventions

The authored balance sheet records trade receivables of $24.734m at 61 collection days, other operating current assets of $18.728m, net fixed/software assets of $43.700m, and historical acquisition goodwill of $138.000m. These are book balances, not collateral recovery estimates. See the model's explicit prepaid/unbilled and deferred-billing simplifications.

- 2025 is comparative income and balance-sheet data; a 2025 cash-flow statement is not fabricated.
- 2026 historical restructuring expense is spread evenly across the four quarters; it is not forecast to recur in 2027.
- Predecessor debt has the same principal and fixed interest rate, with no 2026 amortization; refinancing at par adds no cash or fees.
- 2026 shareholder distributions or contributions reconcile the historical cash target and precede the new agreement.
- Payables and operating accruals (including accrued expenses and deferred customer billings) are held constant; receivables and other operating current assets absorb the stated working-capital change. Deferred customer billings are not forecast separately.
- Other operating current assets comprise prepaid expenses and unbilled contract assets; model movements are explicit, not new EBITDA addbacks.
- Historical goodwill represents earlier acquisitions and stays constant; fixed/software assets roll by capex less depreciation/amortization.
- Restricted cash is borrower-owned cash reserved by contract for operating performance obligations, not client money; it has no matching client-funds liability and no competing lien. It is excluded from leverage netting and available cash.
- Forecast negative cash is an unfunded deficit, not an actual bank balance or assumed new loan; such a scenario is infeasible without corrective financing or spending changes.
- Cash tax is 25% of positive pretax income, with no loss benefit or deferred-tax complexity.
- Q1 realizations are synthetic, with no assumed submission timestamp; every actual delivery obligation must be tested separately.
- Annual interest uses ACT/365F; principal installments are effective December 31, so within-year principal is constant.
