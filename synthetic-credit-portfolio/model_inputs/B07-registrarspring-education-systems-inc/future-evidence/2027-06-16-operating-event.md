# RegistrarSpring Education Systems, Inc. Lakeshore Vocational network consolidation amendment

Synthetic document B07-EV-2027-01. Event date: 2027-06-15. Available to analyst: 2027-06-16.

## P1

On June 15, 2027, RegistrarSpring and Lakeshore Vocational Network executed an amendment consolidating three campuses into two for the coming academic year. The amendment preserves the three-year subscription term, minimum annual platform fee, state-reporting module, and student-record export rights.

## P2

Management reported June 16 that expected student records would decline, but one implementation workstream would be eliminated and the remaining campus migration had a signed milestone plan. No customer termination or nonrenewal notice was issued.
