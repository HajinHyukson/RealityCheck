# B01 demo verification — September 19, 2026

The presentation uses genuine saved NVIDIA-hosted Nemotron outputs. It is a three-scenario development demonstration, not a representative accuracy benchmark. All borrower information, events and amendments are synthetic.

## Model experiments

- Nano Omni 30B was tried first. It identified operating issues but made unreliable covenant mappings and compliance assertions; those attempts are archived and excluded from the presentation.
- Super 120B generated the six-assumption borrower profile: four exact approved memo claims plus two explicitly working inferences. The original source hashes are preserved.
- Ultra 550B was tried on the first scenario with reasoning enabled. It identified the S1 issue but shortened citation locators and missed the calendar roll. The attempt is preserved.
- The final event sequence uses Super 120B with reasoning enabled, operative clauses, contractual definitions and a factual business calendar. Earlier Super attempts are also retained. Prompt and validation adjustments mean these runs are not a controlled model comparison.
