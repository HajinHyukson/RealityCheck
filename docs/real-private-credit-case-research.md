# Real private-credit case research for RealityCheck

Research date: September 19, 2026. This is a source-discovery and suitability assessment, not a completed dataset or a reproduction of confidential underwriting.

**Finding:** sufficient public starting material exists. Recommended cases are RumbleOn/Oaktree for covenant evolution, iRobot/Carlyle for an accessible operating/credit narrative, and PetIQ/Ares for a contrasting set of transaction and definition changes. None supplies a verified complete original underwriting information set. These are document time series, not a ready-made daily loan-price dataset.

## Sufficiency standard

A useful starting case needs an identifiable nonbank/direct-credit facility to an operating company, accessible original terms, multiple dated observations or amendments, and borrower evidence that can be separated by when it became public. A lender fund's own bank borrowing facility does not meet that requirement. A public borrower can still have a private-credit loan.

Distinguish three uses:

- **Historical reconstruction:** public operating evidence plus actual contractual versions and lender responses.
- **Hypothetical underwriting:** any reconstructed original thesis, lender policy or Nemotron-generated alternative package must be labeled as such.
- **Early-detection evaluation:** only information available before the assessment cutoff can be supplied. Later amendments may be observed response evidence after publication, but not input to an earlier forecast of that response.

## 1. iRobot / Carlyle — strong starting case

**Verdict:** sufficient for a public-document reconstruction of a private-credit facility and its evolution. The full internal lender information set is not public. This is a distressed/special-situation case, not a representative sample of ordinary performing private-credit loans.

Carlyle announced the $200 million senior secured loan on July 25, 2023, and explicitly described its private-credit business. This establishes the nonbank lending context rather than inferring it solely from the company being private or public. [Carlyle origination announcement](https://www.carlyle.com/media-room/news-release-archive/carlyle-provides-200-million-term-loan-to-irobot)

### Starting source packet

Use the [Q1 2023 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1159167/000115916723000036/irbt-20230401.htm), origination announcement and original credit agreement for an initial reconstruction. Review the filing index for each document's actual availability before setting cutoffs. Subsequent quarterly filings and the separate amendments provide later packets. Do not initialize from a later amended/conformed agreement and call it the original.

**What is missing:** no confidential Carlyle underwriting memo, complete lender forecast, internal risk policy or full private reporting stream was found. The origination 8-K expressly notes that schedules and exhibits were omitted. Reconstructing a thesis from public materials is possible, but it must remain labeled inferred rather than lender-approved. Some precise calculations may depend on omitted schedules or private inputs.

**Why it fits RealityCheck:** we can compare original protections, subsequent operating evidence, and observed changes in reporting, waivers and lender economics. The original agreement already contemplated merger failure, so the detector must distinguish an anticipated contractual contingency from evidence that an assumed scenario is no longer viable. It must not invent an original assumption that the Amazon deal was certain to close. This suitability judgment is our interpretation of the sources.

**Suggested scope:** first reconstruct July 2023 through June 2025, retaining later filings only for a separate outcome view. This provides an original agreement, a major external event, mixed operating reports and several actual lender responses without requiring the entire bankruptcy process.

## 2. RumbleOn / Oaktree — strongest covenant-history starting point

**Verdict:** sufficient for an actual contract-version and borrower-report replay, with useful published covenant calculations. RumbleOn was an operating powersports retailer. The company identifies $280m of closing term financing from Oaktree-managed funds and other lenders, plus $120m delayed-draw commitments. Keep that facility distinct from its floorplan and consumer financing. [Company closing announcement](https://investors.ridenow.com/rumbleon-announces-the-closing-of-its-business-combination-with-ridenow-creating-the-first-omnichannel-customer-experience-in-powersports/)

**Why it fits:** the sequence supports testing whether apparent compliance reflects improved operations or changed contractual limits. The public EBITDA definition change is a separate comparability issue; do not automatically treat it as a change to contractual EBITDA. This is an interpretation of the source material and a proposed test, not a completed model result.

**Missing:** internal Oaktree underwriting, full private reporting and completed lender compliance certificates were not located. Original contractual terms and the public acquisition rationale can anchor a clearly labeled reconstructed framework. Filing exhibits can contain page images; extracting redlines requires checking inserted/deleted text, not concatenating both versions.

**Suggested initial replay:** the 2023 amendment and reports through Q2 2024, while retaining the 2021 agreement as the origination reference. This is a stronger first contract-comparison dataset than a loan valuation series alone.

## Alternatives screened and insufficiency findings

- **Pluralsight:** public lender schedules provide an impairment/recapitalization series and the borrower confirms the 2024 lender-led recapitalization. Useful for loan marks and outcomes, but the executed 2021 loan and full subsequent covenant amendments were not found in this search. Insufficient for the same original-package comparison as the three cases above. [OCSL June 2024 schedule](https://s206.q4cdn.com/629311627/files/doc_financials/2024/q3/0001414932-24-000012.pdf), [borrower recapitalization announcement](https://www.pluralsight.com/newsroom/press-releases/pluralsight-announces-agreement-to-recapitalize-business). Lender holding values are not total company debt or transaction prices.
- **Benitago / Acrux–CoVenture:** a clean privately held borrower/direct-credit candidate. The [August 31, 2023 first-day declaration](https://cases.stretto.com/public/x233/12096/PLEADINGS/1209608312380000000267.pdf) recounts 2021 origination and upsizes, 2022 waivers, and 2023 forbearance. [Official case archive](https://cases.stretto.com/benitago/) supplies subsequent court reporting. However, the original executed agreement/amendment set and a complete contemporaneous prebankruptcy reporting series were not located. Useful retrospective case; insufficient for a clean pre-event public-data replay without clearly labeled reconstruction.
- **Thrasio:** substantial lender and court records exist, but the financing includes bank and nonbank participants and full original/amendment texts were not verified here. A reconstruction would also have to separate later court narratives from contemporaneous evidence. [2021 borrower financing announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/thrasio-tops-1-billion-in-total-capital-raised-secures-500-million-in-fresh-capital-301204770.html), [official case archive](https://www.veritaglobal.net/thrasio). Lower priority than the directly accessible agreement histories above.

## Recommendation and remaining preparation

Start with **RumbleOn** for the contract-comparison mechanism and **iRobot** for a readily understandable company narrative; use **PetIQ** to test interpretation of changes beyond an obvious deterioration story. This is a recommended selection, not a user-approved change to the current three-company demo.

For each selected case, build a source manifest recording borrower/facility, document URL, document type, covered period, execution/effective dates, public filing timestamp and applicable version. Then extract only a small, coherent clause set and matched operating reports. Full agreement extraction, arithmetic reconciliation and completeness checks remain to be done.

Do not call a reconstruction the lender's actual original thesis, present a generated package as a real amendment, or claim an early warning using evidence published with/after the observed response. Historical company outcomes may also already be known to the model: timestamped retrieval alone cannot eliminate pretrained hindsight. These cases can establish mechanism and source-grounded reconstruction, but need additional controls and unseen cases for performance claims.
