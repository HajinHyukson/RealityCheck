# Crocs — hypothetical private-credit scenario: underwriting memorandum

Fictional scenario document. The borrowing entity, facility, figures and events below were invented for a RealityCheck demonstration. They are not Crocs, Inc. disclosures and no such facility exists.

Memo H01-UW-2026-01. Approved October 1, 2026 by the fictional lender's credit committee for this simulation. Dollar amounts are millions and describe the fictional Loan Parties, not Crocs, Inc.

## Borrower and credit decision

The scenario borrower buys finished footwear from third-party manufacturers, mainly in Vietnam with others in China, Indonesia, India and Mexico, ships it by ocean to distribution centers in the United States and the Netherlands, and sells it through wholesale customers and direct-to-consumer channels. The facility funds the seasonal build of inventory. Repayment depends on inventory turning into cash on schedule, so the package relies on liquidity and reporting protections more than on leverage.

**Sourcing and Logistics.** Lead time from factory to distribution center is about 55 days. Each major origin has at least two ocean routings. About 38% of purchase volume moves on Asia to United States East Coast routings.

**Working Capital.** Inventory of $164.0 million turns in about 120 days. Wholesale receivables of $72.1 million collect in about 46 days. Inventory older than 180 days is 5.5% of inventory at cost.

**Channel Mix.** Wholesale is $572.0 million of revenue and direct-to-consumer is $468.0 million, about 45%.

**Margin.** Goods are bought FOB origin, so the Borrower pays ocean freight. Freight and duty are about 9% of cost of goods.

**Liquidity.** Unrestricted Cash at closing is $64.0 million. The base monthly forecast has a low point of $52.0 million in February 2027.

**Reporting.** Management accounts, cash reports and inventory reports are due within 20 days after each month-end.

## Six approved synthetic underwriting assumptions

### A1

**Claim:** Third-party manufacturers and at least two ocean routings from each major origin keep lead times from factory to distribution center within about 55 days.

**Baseline evidence:** Sourcing and Logistics paragraph of this memo.

**Review criterion:** Lead time exceeds 70 days for at least 10% of purchase volume, or a routing is lost without a working alternative.

**Contract relationship:** C05, C04. These links describe possible protection and do not add a covenant.

### A2

**Claim:** Inventory turns in about 120 days and wholesale receivables collect in about 46 days, so seasonal purchases fund themselves within a season.

**Baseline evidence:** Working Capital paragraph of this memo and the origination financial model.

**Review criterion:** Inventory older than 180 days exceeds 10% of inventory at cost, or wholesale collection exceeds 55 days.

**Contract relationship:** C04, C01. These links describe possible protection and do not add a covenant.

### A3

**Claim:** Direct-to-consumer sales of about 45% of revenue give margin and cash timing that partly offset swings in wholesale orders.

**Baseline evidence:** Channel Mix paragraph of this memo.

**Review criterion:** Confirmed wholesale orders fall by at least $15.0 million in a quarter without a same-period direct-to-consumer offset shown in cash.

**Contract relationship:** C05. These links describe possible protection and do not add a covenant.

### A4

**Claim:** Goods are bought on FOB-origin terms, so the Borrower bears ocean freight; freight and duty are about 9% of cost of goods, and pricing can recover about half of a freight increase within two quarters.

**Baseline evidence:** Margin paragraph of this memo.

**Review criterion:** Freight cost per pair rises by more than 20% for two consecutive months without documented recovery.

**Contract relationship:** C02. These links describe possible protection and do not add a covenant.

### A5

**Claim:** Unrestricted Cash stays above $50.0 million in the base forecast, against a $35.0 million floor.

**Baseline evidence:** Liquidity paragraph of this memo and the base monthly cash forecast.

**Review criterion:** Month-end Unrestricted Cash falls below $45.0 million, or a forecast low point falls below $40.0 million.

**Contract relationship:** C01, C06, C07. These links describe possible protection and do not add a covenant.

### A6

**Claim:** Monthly accounts, cash reports and inventory reports delivered within 20 days give the lender timely visibility of working capital.

**Baseline evidence:** Reporting paragraph of this memo.

**Review criterion:** A required report or notice is late or incomplete.

**Contract relationship:** C03, C04, C05, C06. These links describe possible protection and do not add a covenant.

## Lender policy for this simulation

Fictional policy of the fictional lender: prefer better visibility and reporting before tighter thresholds; grant relief only with compensating protection and a stated end date; never treat unrelated good news as curing a missed obligation. This is a scenario assumption, not any real lender's policy.

## Evidence boundary

Forecasts in this memo are origination forecasts. Later reports are separate documents with their own availability dates and must not be loaded into an origination assessment.
