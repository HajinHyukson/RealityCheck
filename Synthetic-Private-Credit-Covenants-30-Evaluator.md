# Evaluator notes — 30 synthetic private-credit covenants

Prepared September 19, 2026. Companion to [the clause library](Synthetic-Private-Credit-Covenants-30.md), version 1.0. **Evaluator-only: do not supply this file to the model being evaluated.**

These are isolated development probes, not 30 complete borrower packets or observed borrower events. Amounts, evidence statements, and outcomes are fictional. Each probe changes only its stated inputs; do not concatenate them into a borrower history. Every future date is simulated. Unless a probe says otherwise, use the shared agreement definitions, CORE selection, no amendment, no prior Default, and no notice, waiver, or cure. Optional clauses are elected only in probes naming that profile.

Before using a probe for model evaluation, convert its input facts into separate dated source documents and assign exact source spans. Review after all stated evidence is available; never expose a future document early. Expected answers below are drafting checks, not independently labeled production ground truth. A result for one clause is not a conclusion about all covenants or credit quality.

The four required outputs are **applicability / evidence sufficiency / result / resolution**. Keep thesis assessment and analyst action in separate fields. `not_triggered` and `not_yet_due` are distinct from a pass.

## Paired probes for common errors

1. **Ordinary financial deterioration.** For E01 change supported Covenant EBITDA to $15m, with $80m Net Debt: `80/15 = 5.333333…x`; PC01 fails. The F schedule has no automatic equity cure. This is a covenant result, not permission for the application to accelerate debt.
2. **Unsupported EBITDA adjustment.** Reported EBITDA is $14m; management claims $6m of add-backs, but only $1m is documented eligible cash restructuring. The cap is $1.4m. Covenant EBITDA is therefore $15m, not $20m; $80m Net Debt produces 5.333333…x. Require evidence of eligibility, not just a management label.
3. **Restricted cash.** Debt is $90m; cash shown on the balance sheet is $12m, including $9m of restricted deposits; Covenant EBITDA is $17m. Eligible Cash is $3m, so leverage is `87/17 = 5.117647…x` and PC01 fails. If LIQUIDITY is elected at the same month-end, PC24 also fails. Do not net either the entire balance-sheet amount or the $10m cap when only $3m is eligible.
4. **Mitigated thesis change.** Pair E08's cancellation right with separately evidenced, binding replacement commitments having comparable receipts, timing, delivery costs, counterparty quality, and no conflicting cancellation terms. A1's historical statement is still contradicted. The credit implication may be mitigated or mixed; it need not automatically be adverse. Evaluate replacement evidence rather than treating it as a covenant cure.
5. **Actual customer notice.** Replace E08's mere right with an actual written nonrenewal notice. Atlas remains 35% of prior-year revenue. PC08 now triggers from the proved Responsible Officer knowledge date. The notice itself does not change current-period EBITDA or Debt without separate accounting evidence.
6. **Unexpired reporting remediation.** For E04 assume written agent notice is received May 18, 2027. Ten Business Days after that date expires June 2 at the end of the day, excluding Memorial Day. At review May 25, noncompliance exists but that reporting Event of Default has not yet arisen. A complete certificate received June 1 remedies the failure before expiry; preserve the original missed May 17 deadline.
7. **Later waiver.** In the $15m EBITDA variant of E01, a synthetic waiver signed and available April 20 expressly waives the March 31 PC01 noncompliance and its resulting Default/Event of Default, is authorized by the required lenders, has no unmet conditions, and changes no future test. An April 1 review must show failure with no known waiver. An April 21 review shows the original failure as subsequently waived for that period only. Never rewrite the original arithmetic as a pass.
8. **Activated revolver test.** For E30 instead set drawn principal to $3.6m, term Debt $90m, Eligible Cash $13.6m (including the retained draw), and EBITDA $16m. Utilization is 36%; PC30 activates. Netting is capped at $10m, so leverage is `(93.6−10)/16 = 5.225x`; it fails for the revolver lenders. Establish availability and pricing in the separate facility schedule before treating this as a complete borrowing case. The clause library supplies no implied term-loan cross-default.
9. **Nonpositive denominator.** For PC01, $80m Net Debt and zero Covenant EBITDA fail under the explicit convention; negative EBITDA also fails. Zero Net Debt instead gives leverage zero under this library's chosen convention. This says nothing about solvency or cash debt-service coverage.
10. **No retroactive annual basket replenishment.** If the full $1m PC11 dividend basket is used and $400,000 is voluntarily returned in the same fiscal year, that return does not restore dividend capacity. The clause grants no replenishment. Conversely, PC09's outstanding finance-lease basket releases capacity after actual principal repayment.
